01 — Access & Parking

Access & Parking

In Niida, the Iwatake "village of murmuring streams." Check-in is at Square8, across from the Niida Community Center.

📮 Address: 10846 Niida, Hakuba Village, Kitaazumi-gun, Nagano 399-9301
Check-in is at the Square8 reception.
🚃By Public Transport
About a 10-min walk from Shinano-Morikami Station (JR Oito Line).
From Hakuba Station, about 10 min by car. Reach Hakuba Station via highway bus from Nagano (~1 hr) or the Limited Express Azusa from Shinjuku.
🚗Parking
Please use the large car park next to Aburaya Ryokan. When busy, please park temporarily at the Niida Community Center opposite. We'll guide you to parking after check-in.

03 — Room Types

Rooms

Every room has a kitchen, fridge and washer-dryer. Dog-friendly kominka-resort rooms — several with a tatami space.

Kitchen Premium Suite — 6 people
Kitchen Premium Suite
A spacious, open suite with a tatami room (Ichibankan; whole-house booking possible). Up to 6 (1 extra) / approx. 150㎡. Master & guest bedrooms (2 semi-double each) + tatami room (2 futons). Large/medium/small dogs, up to 3.
Kitchen Maisonette Suite — 4 people
Kitchen Maisonette Suite
A maisonette in a renovated storehouse (kura), full of natural-wood warmth. Up to 4 (1 extra) / approx. 75㎡. 2 semi-double beds + tatami room (2 futons). Medium/small dogs, up to 2.
Kitchen Suite — 4 people
Kitchen Suite
A dog-friendly room with a tatami space. Up to 4 / approx. 55–65㎡. 2 semi-double beds + tatami room (2 futons), shower room. Medium/small dogs, up to 2.
Kitchen Junior Suite — 2 people
Kitchen Junior Suite
A cosy room that's just right for two. Up to 2 / approx. 45㎡. 2 semi-double beds, shower room. Small dog, 1.

♨️Mineral Mist Stone Spa @Square8
On the Square8 1st floor. A steam-bath style that lets you absorb concentrated minerals more efficiently than a regular bath — raising your core temperature and helping activate the body.
Hours: 12:00–21:00 / hourly reservation (not private; up to 5 people).
・First-come after check-in. No advance booking — please write your name on the handwritten board by the Square8 front desk.
・1.5 hr per guest (once a day). Five bed spaces.
・Sauna wear & mat are by the dressing room. If you shower in the dressing room, please bring a bath towel from your room.
🧖‍♀️ Note: The stone spa warms the body from within and boosts circulation, so it is not available to: those who have been drinking (enjoy the free alcohol after the spa); those with contagious conditions; those menstruating or pregnant; or small children.

10 — House Rules

House Rules

For everyone's safety and comfort, please observe the following.

  1. The entire property is non-smoking. Please use designated areas.
  2. Please check evacuation routes and avoid anything that could cause a fire.
  3. A lost-key fee of ¥5,000 applies.
  4. Only registered guests may stay; in-room visits are not permitted.
  5. Please manage cash and valuables yourself; we are not liable for theft or loss.
  6. Lost items are kept for 30 days, then handled under applicable law.
